I’m Laura Crawford, a Senior Research Software Engineer at the Rosalind Franklin Institute. Much of my work sits between research, data and infrastructure: helping people get access to the systems they need, improving how research data moves and is described, and working out how the policies behind those processes can be put into practice.

I’m particularly interested in the connections between project administration and digital research infrastructure. A project may be approved in one system, while access to compute, storage or data is managed somewhere else. My Bridging the Gap project explored what it would take to connect those decisions more reliably. I also work on research data workflows and on equity, diversity and inclusion in research and technical communities.

This site is a place to think aloud about that work and the questions it raises. I expect to write about digital research infrastructure, data, AI in research, research practice and EDI, alongside projects and ideas that do not fit neatly into any one category.

The views expressed here are my own.
